## Orders & Payments

GLYVORRA does not collect payments or create customer accounts directly on this website. Orders are placed through WhatsApp, where WhatsApp's own Privacy Policy applies to information handled through its platform.

## Shopping Cart

Your shopping cart is saved locally in your browser using local storage so you can return to your selected products later. Your cart information is not automatically sent to GLYVORRA. It is shared with us only when you choose to place an order through WhatsApp. You can clear your cart at any time by removing items or clearing your browser data.

## Website & Analytics

This website does not currently use its own backend server or database. We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the website, such as which pages are viewed and how they arrive. Google Analytics may set cookies and collect information such as IP address, browser type, device information and usage data, which Google processes under its own privacy policy. Our hosting provider may also automatically collect limited technical information for security and performance purposes.

## Order Information

When you contact us or place an order through WhatsApp, we may use the information you voluntarily share, such as your name, phone number, and delivery address, solely to process, confirm, and deliver your order. We may share necessary delivery details with our shipping partners to fulfil your order.
